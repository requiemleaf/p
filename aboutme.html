Wayne is a Senior UX/Product designer with over 10 years of experience — currently working at Albertsons Companies, Inc.

  • 7+ years of experience in UX/UI design and research within the E-commerce, Retail, and Tech industries.
  • 3+ years of experience in architecture and interaction design.

He has previously collaborated with talents from various companies, brands, and startups including BJ's Wholesale Club, Mosaic, Cubitac, NYU IT, DFA, Etomon, Arcplus, etc. Wayne graduated with a master degree in Integrated Digital Media(year 2020, specializing in UX/ Visual Design) from New York University and hold a master degree in Architecture(year 2015, specializing in Interaction Design).

Coming from an interdisciplinary background, Wayne is a photographer, vlogger, art lover and follower of new techniques. He believes all designs should be built upon research and facts, not assumptions.
